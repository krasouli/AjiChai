# AjiChai
This repository provides reports that has been used in Aji Chai Basin in East Azerbaijan, Iran
The reports include the following: 
- Fakhri‑Fard, A., Majnooni Haris, A., Ahmadzadeh, H., Asghari‑Moghaddam, A., Mousavi, M. M., Ansari, M., Khaledi, M., 2018a. Study and presentation of operational strategies for implementing the resolution to reduce agricultural water use by 40% in the Aji Chai Basin: [Report I – Basin configuration and development of a comprehensive database](https://github.com/krasouli/AjiChai/blob/main/487659_81911_rp1.pdf). Prepared by the University of Tabriz for the Urmia Lake Restoration Committee., 	UT01RE9708035, pp. 143 (In Farsi).
- Fakheri Fard, A., Majnooni, A., Ahmadzadeh, H., Esazadeh, M., 2018b. Study and presentation of operational strategies for implementing the resolution to reduce agricultural water use by 40% in the Aji Chai Basin: [Report II – Soil studies in Aji Chai Basin](https://github.com/krasouli/AjiChai/blob/main/487658_81910_rpt2.pdf). Prepared by the University of Tabriz for the Urmia Lake Restoration Committee, 	UT01RE9708033, pp. 143 (In Farsi)
